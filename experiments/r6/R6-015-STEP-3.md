## How each label is made

**Every certificate and every mutation gets two independent labels, which must agree, or the program stops:**
- **exact rational arithmetic** over the emitted rows, mirroring the SDK's Farkas check at R6's base:
  - every entry names a row;
  - a negative multiplier is allowed only on an equation;
  - the weighted sum must leave no variable;
  - its constant must be positive, or non-negative when a strict row carries a positive multiplier;
- **R6's independent checker** (`verify_certificate`), on the packet.

**Each packet's rows are derived again** with R6's driver, from the packet's input IR. The derived final IR equals the packet's, and
for every learned run the rows equal its retained `prepared.json`.

## The mutation rules

**The rules were fixed in the program before anything was computed.** Wherever a choice is made, entries are taken in sorted
hypothesis-name order, and `neg_goal` is never the chosen entry of rules 2–4 or 8.

**Invalid (control 1).** Each is replayed injected, so the checker's rejection and the closer's outcome are both recorded:
1. `neg_goal_doubled`: `neg_goal`'s multiplier ×2;
2. `inequality_doubled`: the first inequality entry whose doubling makes the arithmetic invalid;
3. `hypothesis_dropped`: the first entry whose removal makes the arithmetic invalid;
4. `unrelated_inequality_added`: the first inequality row outside the map whose addition with multiplier 1 makes the arithmetic
   invalid.

**Validity-preserving (control 2).** Each is replayed through the checked path:

5. `scaled_2`;
6. `scaled_3`;
7. `reordered`: the retained list reversed;
8. `zero_entry_added`: the first row outside the map, with multiplier 0;
9. at l166, `hrec_dropped` from the deterministic map. It equals the learned map, as the proposal said.

**Every rule had a candidate for every map, and every label agrees.**
- **The 24 invalid mutations:** each leaves a non-cancelling residual. For example, l204's `neg_goal` doubled leaves
  `−a + b + 2`. The checker reports the same sum (`farkas_not_contradictory`).
- **The 25 valid mutations:** each is verified by both labels.
- **The chosen entries:**
  - rule 2: `hlt` at l166, `hz0` at l175, `this` at l178, `hab` at l204;
  - rule 3: `hlt`, `hrec`, `this` and `hab` respectively;
  - rules 4 and 8: `_pb_nonneg_Zmax` at every map.
