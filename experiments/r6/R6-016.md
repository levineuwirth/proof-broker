## The l070 draw 5 diagnosis

**The frozen program ran after the replay, under the lock,** on R6's retained export, the first time R6-016 read it. In the export's
`0 < s` it found three atoms, and **one pair** that prints identically but is not equal: atoms 1 and 2, each `Zmax * zhigh.val`, a ℕ
product under a cast.

**The difference** (the first differing subterm) is `ZMod.val`'s modulus argument:
- atom 1 has the numeral `18446744069414584321`;
- atom 2 has the constant `Bracket.P`.

**The attempts:**

| attempt | outcome |
|---|---|
| printed | equal by default; different under `pp.all` |
| definitional, reducible and instances (with and without `zetaDelta`) | unsuccessful |
| definitional, default (both) | **established**, confirmed by the kernel |
| the kernel's `rfl` | **established** |
| `omega`, `grobner` | unsuccessful: each treats them as two atoms |
| counterexample | refused: `zhigh : ZMod Bracket.P` is outside ℕ and ℤ, as anticipated |

**The classification is `printed_only`.** The two occurrences are the same number: one has `Bracket.P` unfolded to its numeral,
one does not.
- **Why they stay apart:** R6-016's reifier identifies atoms at instance transparency, where `Bracket.P` does not unfold, so the
  sum keeps two atoms and does not cancel. R6-015's ring normalizer and the qualification audit's `omega` met the same split.
- **Read with the disclosed limit:** the export carries no attributes, so the failure at reducible and instance transparency is
  established for the export's environment. The replay, which ran in the site's own environment, failed at instance transparency
  as well.

The diagnosis changes no route and counts toward nothing.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**It establishes:**
- **R6's refusal at the four obligations is removable under R6's full acceptance,** by the specified reconstruction changes:
  - selection by the goal's carrier;
  - fact assertion at `Int`;
  - a final step that cannot draw on context and adds no axiom.
- **Every retained certificate was consumed in the strong sense:** its weighted sum alone proves the step, hypothesis-free and
  kernel-checked, at both targets.
- **Invalid certificates failed there,** and valid mutations were consumed.
- **R6-015's remaining boundary was exactly the two things it named:** the final step's axiom footprint, and the audit's
  parameter count at l175. Both are removed, and nothing else surfaced.

**It does not establish:**
- **Generality.** These are four obligations, in two families of one file, as the proposal said.
- **That the normalizer is complete.** It identifies atoms only up to instance-transparent definitional equality, without
  commutativity or associativity. The disclosed limits stand:
  - l070 draw 5's split;
  - `↑(a * b) * ↑a` against `↑(a * b * a)` (the bridge's interpretation 3);
  - R6's checker rejecting the cast-of-product identity that the route accepts (control 9c).
- **Anything about R6-014's or R6-015's numbers,** which stand as recorded.
