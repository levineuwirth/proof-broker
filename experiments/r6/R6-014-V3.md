## 1. A progress floor (finding 1, P1; cohort v9)

A **progress floor**, `<policy>.floor.json`, sits beside the policy and outside the ledger directory. It holds the live ledger's row
count and last row hash. It is written before the activation receipt and advanced after every durable append.

Every read of the live ledger (snapshot, reservation, reconciliation, revision) requires the ledger to extend the floor:
- **Behind the floor, or diverging at its position:** `cohort_ledger_rolled_back`. This happens before any write, for `sign`,
  `authorize`, `revise`, `live_permitted` and a reservation.
- **A receipt present but no floor:** lost authority.
- **Ahead of the floor:** an append whose floor update was interrupted. The floor catches up.

Controls:
- **`ledger_rollback_refused`.** This is the review's scenario: an activation-only snapshot of the same ledger restored over a spent
  one, with the receipt unchanged. It also covers a same-length divergence and a removed floor. Every entry is refused, and restoring
  the preserved ledger serves the same consumption.
- **`ledger_ahead_of_floor_recovers`.**

The reviewer's authority probe now stops at `cohort_ledger_rolled_back`.

**Limit, restated.** The floor and the ledger are both operator-retained files. If *both* are restored to the same earlier state, the
floor also going back, the rollback cannot be detected locally. Binding progress against that would need an external witness, which is
not part of this checkpoint.

## 2. Gzip coverage from the bytes (finding 2, P1)

Every inventory entry's required streams are derived from the bytes it names, by the frozen scanner's own rule: a raw stream always,
and a gzip stream exactly when the bytes begin `1f 8b`. The file name plays no part. Each entry's file must exist at its recorded digest
and size, and the aggregate `gzip_streams_scanned` must agree.

Controls:
- the review's compressed `.bin` reported as raw-only. Its precondition is recorded: the frozen scanner finds gzip-stream disclosures
  in the file and none in its raw stream;
- a non-run file changed after the scan;
- a miscounted gzip total.

All are rejected at `operator_scan:report_bound`.

## 3. Deterministic evidence joins (findings 3–5, P2; auditor revision 2)

- **Observation grammar.** Every run's search observations follow the frozen grammar for its route and outcome: event, component and
  order.
- **Certificate agreement.** Every certificate-bearing observation carries the certificate the bridge received. The SDK's
  `certificate_created` differs only by the unbound configuration digest, which `certificate_bound` fills in exactly.
- **Re-checked verification.** Wherever the bridge claims verification, refused runs included, the packet is rebuilt from the
  observations and the independent checker is **re-executed**.
- **Pinned closer.** The selected closer is derived from the pinned dispatch for this IR and certificate.
- **Solution join.** The retained solution's decompressed bytes must equal the retained export stdout and its sealed digest. Its size
  must equal the export's recorded output and receipt, and it must contain the site's local and whole declarations. That the replays
  read these bytes rests on the stage commands and the chain, not on re-running them.
- **Search cleanup.** Every search stage must have terminated cleanly with an empty workload. Only its exit code may carry a
  refusal, and a proof requires exit 0.
- **Case population.** The complete case population is computed per outcome and gated (`audit:case_population_complete`).

Controls: the review's probes are all rejected. They are the zero witness on a refused run (with the checker's rejection shown first),
the relabelled closer, the altered verification observation, the non-empty workload, a non-zero proof exit, and another site's export
substituted.
