## Why 18 do not count

**1. Acceptance 4, "axioms unchanged".**
- **The cause.** The constrained final step's proof (`TermMode.posOfNormNum`) depends on `Classical.choice`, through the soundness
  proof of core's ring normalizer.
- **Where it adds an axiom.** Where the obligation's original proof did not use `Classical.choice`, the constrained proof adds it:
  - **l166 and l175:** to the local target. Their whole declaration, `cell_value_neutral`, already used it.
  - **l096 and l099:** to both targets, in control 5.
- **Where it does not.** l178 and l204, like l069 to l078, already depended on `Classical.choice`, and their axioms are unchanged.
- **The effect.** All of l166's and l175's proofs, retained certificates and valid mutations alike, fail acceptance 4.

**This is my error, and the record should say so.** The build records stated that both routes' axioms are "within acceptance 4".
That is true of the allowed set (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`), but it is not true of "unchanged" at an obligation
whose original proof did not use `Classical.choice`. I did not check that before the lock. The frozen analysis checks both halves,
as it should.

**2. Control 8 at l175.** The audit program locates the whole target by requiring the whole declaration to refer to the local
theorem exactly once, at full arity. At l175 it refers to it once, but with 14 arguments for the local's 13 parameters.
- l175 is a post-`simp` site, and its source applies the captured result to one more argument.
- **The whole target is therefore not locatable**, at all 17 l175 proofs.
- **Their local targets are all `certificate_alone`,** with no hypothesis referenced.
- R6's own audit never met l175, since R6 had no proofs there.

So l175 fails twice, on control 8 and on acceptance 4. l166 fails on acceptance 4 only, and passes control 8.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**It establishes:**
- R6's refusal at the four obligations was removable by the specified reconstruction changes: selection by goal carrier, fact
  assertion at `Int`, and a final step that cannot draw on context;
- every retained certificate became a kernel-accepted proof under that step;
- invalid certificates failed there;
- at l178 and l204, both sources' certificates were consumed and validated under every frozen check.

**It does not establish complete success.** The boundary it locates is not where R6 stopped:
- **the final step's axiom footprint,** which changes the axioms at obligations whose original proofs avoided `Classical.choice`;
- **one site's whole-target locatability** in the audit program.

## Erratum, 2026-10-02

**The account of l175's whole target above is wrong in its cause.** Section 2 of "Why 18 do not count" says l175's source
"applies the captured result to one more argument", and "Proposed, not started" calls the gap over-application. Neither is so.
- **The cause** is a `have` partway through the local theorem's closed type:
  `… → ∀ (hzh : ¬zhigh = 0), have hzh1 := ⋯; ↑Zmax ≤ ↑Zmax * zhigh → ↑Zmax ≤ v`. R6's capture helper keeps let-bound entries in
  the closed telescope and does not apply them (`capture/CaptureSite.lean`).
- **So the whole declaration** applies the local to its 14 non-let binders, which is the right number.
  `qualification-audit-v1` counted 13 parameters, because `forallTelescope` stops at the first `let`.

This was found in drafting [amendment 1 to the audit](R6-QUALIFICATION-1-AUDIT-AMENDMENT-1-PROPOSAL.md), and its erratum was
proposed there.

**R6-015's result is unchanged.** Control 8 ran, as frozen, with `qualification-audit-v1`, and stays "not as frozen" at l175.
Under the amended program (`qualification-audit-v2`), all 17 l175 proofs are locatable at both targets, through 14 established
parameters, and classify as certificate alone at both. That record is informational only
([R6-015-L175-AUDIT-V2.md](R6-015-L175-AUDIT-V2.md)). l175's proofs also fail acceptance 4, and that is unaffected.
