## The three findings and their repairs

1. **Interrupted directory creation defeated the durability repair on retry
   (P2, locked).** `cohort_ledger.reserve` no longer derives what to sync
   from `path.exists()`: on every reservation it creates the task and draw
   directories with `exist_ok` and the attempt directory exclusively, then
   fsyncs the whole authority ancestry — attempt, draw, task, `slots`, the
   ledger directory — deepest first and each parent after its child, before
   the row is appended. A directory left by an attempt that failed before
   its ancestors were synced is synced again by the next attempt. Controls:
   `slot_hierarchy_durable` now requires the full ancestry, in order, for a
   first task/first draw and again for a new draw under an existing task;
   `slot_ancestry_durable_after_interrupted_creation` injects one fsync
   failure at the task directory and, independently, at `slots`: the first
   attempt raises with no row appended and the directories left behind; the
   retry appends the reservation and its completed fsyncs cover the whole
   ancestry in child-before-parent order.
2. **The auditor accepted a zero reservation against a 102,400 µUSD
   admission (P2, audit).** New per-run case
   `ledger:reservation_priced_from_contract_limits`: the permit's
   reservation equals the price of the frozen token limits at the admitted
   rates (`cost_micro(input_tokens_reserved, output_tokens, rates)`), equals
   the rederived host admission's and the actor check's amounts, its
   message size is the recomputed envelope's, and `reservation.json`
   carries the same values; the actor check also joins its amount to the
   permit. Campaign committed money is now derived from those checked
   amounts, not from the rows' own figures. Control
   `zero_reservation_coherent` carries a zero amount through permit, ledger
   rows, head, both snapshots, reservation, accounting, summary and receipts
   and is rejected at that case.
3. **Execution and receipt claims detached from evidence (P2, audit).**
   Three repairs, each with the review's controls:
   - *Mounted implementation identity.* `mounts:authority_bound` binds every
     module mount source to the recorded repository root — the directory
     whose campaign ledger the sender mounted — and requires the driver's
     recorded roles (`actor_source`, `ledger_module`, `network_stage`,
     `ledger_directory`, `contract`) to name the same files under that
     root, the recorded runtime and interpreter to appear in the command,
     and the records path to be the run's own. The retained provenance
     copies are those files (`modules:*`). No original absolute directory is
     required to exist. Control `mounted_adapter_foreign` (approved basename
     from an unapproved directory) rejects there.
   - *Successful execution versus terminated workload.* New per-run case
     `receipts:stage_returned_within_frozen_limits`: the sender's process
     record shows exit 0, no exhaustion or violation, monitor and
     observation success and an empty workload; the command's wall, CPU,
     memory and output limits are the policy's; the start receipt names
     those limits. Controls `nonzero_sender_exit_coherent` (exit 7 rebound
     through the finish receipt, reconciliation evidence, ledger, head,
     snapshot and receipts) and `resource_limit_receipt_wrong` reject there.
   - *Receipt meaning versus receipt presence.* New per-run case
     `receipts:payloads_bound_to_records`: every supervisor receipt payload
     is reconstructed from the record it names and compared exactly — each
     stage's start (limits from its command) and finish (its process
     record), `episode_started`, both `payload_validated` receipts,
     `reservation_attempted`, `pricing_admitted`, `request_reserved`,
     `reservation_reconciled`, `https_observed`, `transport_validated`,
     `credential_receipt_checked`, `reservation_refused`, both
     `kernel_verdict` receipts. `credential_receipt:recorded` now binds the
     expected hash to the mounted canary's header and the observed hash to
     the receiver's captured authorization digest. Controls: the five false
     receipt payloads reject at the payload case; `credential_hash_pair_false`
     at the receipt case.

## Audit revision 2, after the review of `7240ae5`

The [review](reviews/2026-09-14/R6-009-V3-REVIEW.md) confirmed the three
repairs and every figure above, and found two gaps in the non-locked
auditor; both are repaired in `cohort_audit.py` without a new lock or native
rerun. The lock, policies, runs and ledger are as recorded above.

1. **Runtime and mount population.** New per-run case
   `command:reconstructed_from_pinned_runtime_and_layout`: the whole
   recorded sender command is rebuilt — the sandbox prefix, every library
   mount from the pinned runtime record's inventory (the retained
   `campaign-runtime-v1.json` whose digest is the policy's
   `runtime_lock_sha256`, equal to the retained `python-runtime.json` and
   named by the recorded roles), the stdlib as that pin's materialization
   under the recorded repository root, the pinned interpreter as the
   program, the six module and seven record mounts, the ledger, the fixture,
   the four ephemeral credential/TLS mounts (the only unconstrained
   sources; they may not lie under the root or the run directory, whereas
   the interpreter and library sources also lie outside the repository but
   are fixed by the pin), the two writable mounts, and the actor's argument
   vector —
   and compared with the record exactly, so nothing else can be mounted. No
   original directory needs to exist.
2. **Every stage on the path.** New per-run case
   `stages:every_stage_on_the_path_returned`: the retained stage population
   is exactly the outcome's frozen path (three stages for a refusal, four
   for a pre-send release, eleven for a proof), and each stage's process
   record shows a zero exit, no exhaustion or violation, monitor and
   observation success and an empty workload, under the sender's policy
   limits or the frozen `episode.stage` defaults, with event capture only
   for reconstruction.
