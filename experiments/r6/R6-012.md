separates three questions per site:

  Nonexistence is never reported without that point.
- **Name addressability.** The name-addressed checker *refuses* an ambiguous row set rather than choosing a row.

## The blocking finding: the preparation helper reifies differently from reconstruction

Reconstruction refuses a proposal unless its input IR equals the IR that the proof tactic reifies afresh. The frozen
R6-001 preparation helper does not compute that IR the way the term-mode tactic does, in two respects:

1. **Tier directive.** The helper sets the tier preference with `ir.userDirectives.map …`, which does nothing when the
   reifier emitted no directives. The tactic merges it with `getD {…}`, which creates the directive
   (`lean-bridge/ProofBroker/Tactic.lean`, `buildExtractionPath`). This affects all five failing sites.
2. **Identifier sanitization.** The tactic alpha-renames identifiers that are not SMT-safe before reifying (R4.2:
   `c'` becomes `c_`); the helper reifies the raw names. This additionally affects l096 and l099. For those sites the
   model is shown `c'` while the SDK and Lean address `c_`.

Both defects were latent since R6-001: D1 and C8 both carry rewriter directives and SMT-safe names. The four passing
sites are exactly the ones with directives and safe names.

**Attribution.** This is a limit of the learned arm's delivery path, and says nothing about the certificates (all
nine are exact). The deterministic arm is not implicated, because the broker's own route reifies once through
`buildExtractionPath`. Its outcomes are to be measured, not inferred from this.

**Proposed repair (next locked revision).** The site preparation helper should reify exactly as term mode does:
1. apply the R4.2 renaming;
2. run `buildIR`;
3. merge the tier preference with `getD`.

The prepared input IR then changes for the five affected sites. For l096 and l099 the variable names the model sees
change from `c'` to `c_`, which are the names the SDK and Lean use. This needs:
- site harness v3 (the helper);
- representability v3 (re-prepared problems, reclassified);
- a cohort v5 freeze on the new prepared problems;
- rerunning the site rehearsals.

The expectation is that all nine certificate sites then reconstruct. That is to be demonstrated, not assumed.

**Not done in this checkpoint.** The historical auditor has not been extended to the v4 runs. It is deferred to the
repaired revision, whose runs are the ones that will serve as evidence. The v4 runs are retained as the observation of
the defect.
