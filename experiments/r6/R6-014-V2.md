## 1. Lost live authority fails closed (finding 1, P1; cohort v8)

Live activation now completes only with a durable **activation receipt**, `<policy>.activation.json`, written beside the policy and
outside the ledger directory. The activation row also carries a random identity, so a fresh activation can never match the receipt.

With the receipt present, a ledger that is missing, headless or different is refused rather than re-activated. The refusal code is
`cohort_activation_authority_lost`, and it applies to `sign`, `authorize`, `revise` and `live_permitted`. No write happens first, and
the lost directory is not recreated.

A live reservation requires the receipt, and the receipt must name the ledger's activation row. Consumption therefore implies the
receipt existed. Without a receipt, activation never completed, and nothing was ever reserved under it. That is why an interrupted
first activation can still resume safely.

Controls:
- **`lost_authority_fails_closed`.** This is the review's scenario. It first shows the spent-slot refusal. It then removes the
  established authority in four ways: the whole live directory, the ledger and head (slots kept), the head alone, and a fresh
  activation in place. Every re-entry is refused. Restoring the preserved directory serves the same consumption again.
- **`live_reservation_requires_activation_receipt`.** Reservations are refused with no receipt, with a foreign receipt, and from an
  unbound ledger.
- **Interrupted signing.** Two new boundaries were added and resume: after activation but before the receipt, and a torn receipt.
  The torn receipt is refused until an operator removes it; the ledger is untouched.

The reviewer's own probe (`r6_014_review_probes.py quick`) now stops at re-signing with `cohort_activation_authority_lost`.

**Limit.** A receipt is a file, like the ledger. If both the receipt and the ledger are lost, the loss cannot be told apart from never
having signed. Both are operator-retained records, and the scan and audit cover the ledger.

## 2. The auditor accepts the production signing state (finding 2, P1)

- **Signed state.** A signed revision is its checkpoint except for its authority, schedule, money, revision chain and pricing. Its
  schedule must lie within the plan.
- **Pricing per revision.** Each revision's pricing is bound to its own retained capture: the admission review is recomputed with

- **Revision history** (`revision:history_bound`). Every retained revision (`<policy>.r<n>.json`) is checked:

  - the policy directory holds exactly the policy, its lock, the checkpoint, the receipt and the retained revisions.
- **Runs and ledger.** Each run is bound to the revision it was reserved under. The live ledger carries exactly those revisions, in

## 4. Deterministic-arm artifact audit (finding 4, P2)

The new [`deterministic_audit.py`](reviews/2026-09-23/deterministic_audit.py) is a non-locked auditor over the retained runs; no episode
was rerun. It checks:
- **Population:** an exact population of 15 sites × 2 routes, taken from the reviewed primary population rather than the summary;
- **Seals:** every retained file at its sealed digest;
- **Provenance:** locked-source provenance and binaries at the current tool digests;
- **Inputs:** recomputed;
- **Commands:** each stage command rebuilt by the production `site_stage.stage` with its launch stubbed, and compared byte for byte;
- **Receipts:** each stage receipt equal to its process record;
- **Search outcome:** recomputed;
- **Proofs:**
  - the captured context is frozen;
  - the evidence packet is rebuilt from the observations;
  - the certificate check is **re-executed**;
  - the consumption receipt (arm) or closer receipt (reference) is bound;
  - the retained solution's bytes are bound;
  - both kernel replays are **derived from their raw reports**, down to the frozen type digest and the standard axioms.

Records: [audit](reviews/2026-09-23/R6-014-V2-DETERMINISTIC-AUDIT.json) (478 cases, all 30 runs) and
