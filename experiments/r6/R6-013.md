## 3. Closer-specific consumption receipts (site harness v4, cohort v6)

**The overlay.** [`consumption_overlay.py`](consumption_overlay.py) applies the frozen overlay unchanged and then adds
observations only:

**The driver** (cohort v6):
- **Proof.** Requires the closer's own receipt, naming this certificate and the selected closer, before accepting a
  proof. The verdict takes the closer and consumption fields from that receipt.
- **Refusal.** Records the pinned ℕ closer's refusal as `reconstruction_refused`, with diagnosis `nat_closer_int_goal`.
  The diagnosis is derived only from bound evidence:
  - the dispatched IR equals the packet;
  - the certificate was verified;
  - `term_mode_nat` was selected for a comparison that is not at ℕ;
  - the process exited 1 with no exhaustion, violation, monitor or observation failure;
  - there is no success receipt;
  - there is exactly one error, and it is that closer's message.

  Anything else remains an undifferentiated stage failure. Two cohort controls exercise seven receipt variants and eleven
  refusal variants, including a v5 record without branch evidence (refused).

The tactic is unchanged. A closer repair would be a separate method comparison.

## 7. Review repairs (auditor revision 2)

The review found that six coherent mutations passed all 628 cases. Each repair is a new or strengthened predicate in
[`cohort_v6_audit.py`](reviews/2026-09-22/cohort_v6_audit.py) (docstring: revision 2):

1. **Refused reconstruction's evidence bindings.** `reconstruction:evidence_bound` runs on both the proof and the refused path, before the
   closer is examined. It requires these to equal this run's packet:
   - every certificate-bearing child observation;
   - the dispatched certificate, final IR and trace;
   - the dispatch settings;
   - the observation sequence and each child's stage and component boundary.

   The packet's final IR must equal the prepared problem's. A valid final verification no longer excuses a contradictory earlier
   observation.
2. **Closer attribution by derivation.** The expected closer is derived from the pinned `runTermModeOnGoal` predicates over the
   dispatched IR and certificate: `natModeOf` (a ℕ free variable or a `nat_nonlinear_atom` payload), `polyModeOf`, the case-split hint,
   and the extension registry. The registry is empty: the reconstruction loads exactly the six core bridge modules, and neither input
   imports `ProofBrokerMathlib`. The selected closer must equal the derived one; receipt, verdict and refusal must equal the selection.
   Relabeling every mirror together now rejects.
3. **Stage commands rebuilt.** `stages:commands_reconstructed` rebuilds every non-sender stage command (preparation, pipeline, assembly,
   certificate check, capture build, reconstruction, export and both replays) as `site_stage.stage` builds it. The inputs are that
   stage's call, the pinned D1 toolchain and environment, the revision 2 bridge and this run's own paths, and the comparison is exact.
   Two parts are host- or run-dependent and are bounded instead:
   - the shared-library closure must be system libraries at their own paths, equal for the same stage across runs;
   - the challenge must be one verified temporary copy for both replays.
4. **Historical kernel successes bound to their reports.** The raw replay reports are normalized as final validation normalizes them.
   For v5's proofs, v5's qualified l096/l099 and v4's four proofs, they must equal the verdict and both kernel receipts, name the site's
   declarations, keep the frozen types and add no axiom. The explicit missing-receipt qualification of v5's l096/l099 is kept.

The shared proof checker now uses the same replay-report helper.
